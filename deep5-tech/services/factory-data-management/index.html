Frequently asked questions

Practical answers before starting your Factory Data Systems build.

Will machine operators need fast typing skills to log entries?

No. Simple tap selectors, batch dropdowns, and QR job-card scans keep logging under ten seconds.

Can it work if plant Wi-Fi drops temporarily?

Yes. Screens queue local submissions cleanly and sync automatically once signal returns.

Can we trace a customer complaint back to the raw material batch?

Yes. Full lot genealogy links final boxes back to heat numbers, machines, and QC inspectors.

Does this integrate with our existing ERP or HRMS?

Yes. We connect attendance shifts and stock ledgers directly over secure APIs.
