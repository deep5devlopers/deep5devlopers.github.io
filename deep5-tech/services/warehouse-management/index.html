Frequently asked questions

Practical answers before starting your Warehouse Management build.

Do we need expensive proprietary barcode hardware to run this WMS?

No. Standard USB/Bluetooth scanners and regular Android phones scan labels accurately.

Can the system manage inventory across multiple warehouses?

Yes. View stock per godown, reserved dispatch orders, and inter-branch transfers in one dashboard.

Does your WMS handle batch FIFO and shelf-life tracking?

Yes. Picklists highlight oldest valid batches automatically to prevent dead stock buildup.

Can we export packing slips and low-stock reorder lists to Excel?

Yes. Every stock movement ledger exports cleanly to CSV, Excel, and print PDFs.
